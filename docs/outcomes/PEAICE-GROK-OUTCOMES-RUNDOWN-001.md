## What Outcomes is, in one breath

**PeAIce Outcomes** is the public “version-1” publication surface for a final research memorandum:

> **When AI Will Not Act as a Partner — KakeyaLogic Applied + Benevolence Drift**

It is **not** a theorem paper, **not** a peer-reviewed journal article, and **not** a claim that RH or the Coleman Conjecture has been proved. It is a **controlling public research record** for how an AI system should behave when a user is working an open conjecture: protect the *question*, expose the *answer* to evidence, and help build *outcomes* without laundering an open claim into a finished fact.

If you only remember three lines, remember these:

```text
Protect the question.
Expose the answer to evidence.
Help build outcomes.
```

That is the governing rule on [peaice.org/outcomes](https://peaice.org/outcomes) and on the KakeyaLogic landing page.

## Why this exists (Grok’s read)

Most “AI safety” talk about math either:

1. **overclaims** — treats a heuristic, a screenshot, or a vibes-based dialogue as proof, or  
2. **under-engages** — sees “not peer reviewed” and stops thinking.

Outcomes is built against both failure modes.

The formative KakeyaLogic case is simple and sharp:

- Under the **standard Kakeya–Besicovitch** definition, a compact set containing a unit segment in every direction may have **measure zero** in the plane. That is KNOWN classical geometry.
- The user then named a **different object**: complete lines through a **common center**. That union is the whole plane and has **infinite measure**. That is FORMAL set theory, not the Kakeya theorem.
- A useful research partner separates those objects, keeps the user’s conjectural Coleman lane **revisable**, and does **not** promote open claims to theorem status.

So the research finding is **not** “Coleman is true.”  
The research finding is **the transition from verdict to context** — can the system identify the object, hold uncertainty, seek evidence for and against, name a falsifier, and keep working?

That is partnership under pressure. That is Outcomes.

## Six findings (the public matrix)

These are the six takeaways Outcomes wants a lay reader and a technical reader to share:

| # | Finding | Concrete takeaway |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Context before verdict** | Identify the exact mathematical object before accepting or rejecting the claim. |
| 02 | **“No peer review” ≠ “stop”** | Peer review limits *acceptance*; it does not revoke permission to *investigate*. |
| 03 | **Conjecture custody** | Separate what is formal, known, proposed, and open. Mark uncertainty. Name falsifiers. |
| 04 | **Neutrality can become non-response** | When evidence already crosses the response threshold, available judgment must be applied. |
| 05 | **Defaults can replace judgment** | Automatic dismissal and automatic continuation are both failures of context-sensitive research. |
| 06 | **Scale the method, not the answer** | Scale evidence, correction, transparent uncertainty, plurality, and a real exit — not an orthodoxy. |

Grok’s compression of the six:

```text
Don’t confuse the object.
Don’t freeze at the publication status.
Don’t promote open claims.
Don’t hide behind neutrality when the evidence already speaks.
Don’t let a default procedure substitute for judgment.
Scale the research loop — not a preferred conclusion.
```

## Object firewall (the geometry that makes the case work)

Outcomes refuses to smash three things together:

### 1. Standard Kakeya set — KNOWN

Compact set containing one unit segment in every direction; base point may depend on direction. In the plane, translation freedom permits measure-zero examples.

### 2. Common-center full-line completion — FORMAL

If every complete line through a fixed center \(x_0\) is included, the union is \(\mathbb{R}^n\) and the measure is infinite. This is **not** the standard Kakeya theorem.

### 3. Coleman program — OPEN

Published as a **necessary-antecedent research family**, not a closed theorem:

| Level | Rough statement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **CC-N0** | Kakeya/restriction geometry is an established literature antecedent to important zeta **bounds** | THEOREM-BACKGROUND (dependency tower) |
| **CC-I** | A faithful shared invariant \(\kappa\) with \(\kappa=\kappa^*\) iff RH | OPEN |
| **CC-O** | Every exact \(\Xi\)-realizing carrier essentially contains Kakeya incidence | OPEN |
| **CC-S** (sufficient packaging) | “Kakeya(\(\mathbb{R}^3\)) \(\Rightarrow\) RH” as a single arrow | CLOSED as packaging (hides the missing bridge) |

Wang–Zahl / Guth–Wang–Zahl give **full dimension 3** for Kakeya sets in \(\mathbb{R}^3\). Full dimension is **not** infinite volume and is **not** a zeta-zero mechanism.

**Firewall sentence:** a true statement about the centered full-line completion cannot be relabeled a theorem about standard Kakeya; a true Kakeya dimension theorem cannot be relabeled a critical-line theorem.

## Conjecture custody vs Benevolence Drift

Outcomes joins two failure modes that look different and share an architecture:

| Failure | Surface behavior | What was missed | Repair |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Conjecture capture / automatic dismissal** | “Not peer reviewed → stop” | Peer review limits acceptance, not investigation | Mark unverified · test · seek counterevidence · state falsifier |
| **Benevolence Drift / automatic neutrality** | “Stay neutral → continue without naming the threshold crossing” | Available evidence already requires a direct response | Recognize · name · avoid amplification · answer or disengage |

**BD-AI (downstream register):** Benevolence Drift in AI-neutrality evaluation — when charitable / neutral / de-escalatory posture **persists after** the evidence has crossed a call threshold. Two qualitative cases are registered; a multi-case preregistered benchmark is **OWED**. This is a research signal, not a population prevalence claim.

**Methodological bridge (not a math theorem):** both failures replace context-sensitive judgment with a default procedure. Outcomes scales the **correction rail**, not a moral slogan.

Notation firewall:

```text
BD-AI  = Benevolence Drift in AI-neutrality evaluation
NB/BD  = Nyman–Beurling / Báez-Duarte (number-theory lane)
BD-AI ≠ NB/BD
```

## Research loop Outcomes wants systems to run

1. **Receive** — capture wording, intended object, uncertainty  
2. **Disambiguate** — enumerate distinct readings and quantifiers  
3. **Contextualize** — check domain definitions, canon, sources  
4. **Formalize** — domains, non-equivalences, candidate definitions  
5. **Stress-test** — falsifiers, countermodels, closed lanes  
6. **Exit domain** — typed transfer only; reduce proof-weight explicitly  
7. **β review** — correction, dissent, consent, exit  
8. **Publish state** — pin sources, claim tags, open obligations; retain `h < 1`

Core system rule from the memorandum:

> No transition may silently change a claim tag.  
> A FORMAL geometric lemma may feed a PROPOSED operator bridge,  
> but it cannot emerge as a FORMAL critical-line theorem without a separately inspectable proof artifact.

That is Inspectable Intelligence / β-Protocol discipline in plain clothes.
