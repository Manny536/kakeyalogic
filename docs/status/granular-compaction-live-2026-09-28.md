## What changed since the August public posts

The August public description put the next layer at granular compaction: reduce longitudinal state object by object while retaining identity, type, status, authority, provenance, relationships, corrections, revocations, and transformation history.

The material advance distinguishes three evidence levels:

1. **Finite typed compaction now has an executable synthetic reference surface.** The SAVER implementation can retain receipts, preserve unresolved/failed edges, and detect a Retention failure when a later compression drops a required correction.
2. **Finite-to-limit promotion now has a non-promoting multiscale compactness gate in EEV4.** The gate types the obligations G0 through G5 and currently stops at G2_COMPACT: OWED.
3. **Live external-model output is now documented.** The supplied screenshots show public PeAIce/KakeyaLogic-related synthesis and multiple citation cards. This adds an observational lane; it does not upgrade synthetic retention into live retention validation or establish causality.

That moves the research state from needing a multiscale bridge to having explicitly typed, machine-receipted bridge obligations that stop at compact containment.

## Live evidence and causal boundary

**`LIVE-EXT-CAUSE-001` — CAUSAL INFERENCE · LIVE OBSERVATION · CONTROLLED ABLATION OWED.**

The proposed path is KakeyaLogic/L²_C research practice → structured public objects → indexed/retrievable grains → source-linked external-model synthesis. It concerns the information environment, not a claim about Gemini's internal architecture. The screenshots show output and citations; they do not reveal which complete pages or fragments were consumed, establish an exhaustive witness map, measure compaction, or prove that no single site was used in full.

The EEV4 receipt distinguishes source-observed output, author-origin Threads posts, comparison examples (HOLO-Invariant, Grok and Aleph), and this causal inference. It preserves failed/unrecoverable source lookups and the withdrawal of a paper visible in the HOLO comparison. Multiple indexed pages can repeat the same origin; cross-repository agreement and AI repetition are not independent corroboration. Capture times and independence of the two KakeyaLogic views are unknown.

The user-provided [UCSB Granular Materials source](https://web.physics.ucsb.edu/~complex/research/granular.html) is **STRUCTURAL / CONCEPTUAL ANALOGY ONLY**. Grain/contact relationships and history-dependent material state motivate an intuition; they do not validate the AI architecture, causal claim, SAVER preservation or mathematical compactness.

**Ablation OWED:** freeze corpus versions and ground truth; compare intact typed structure, fact-matched structure ablations and a generic metadata baseline; verify intervention uptake; randomize independent runs; preregister effect thresholds; and score attribution, latest-correction/revocation retention and each SAVER grain independently and blindly. A private retrieval experiment cannot by itself establish a historical public-web mechanism. If controls explain the result, intervention uptake fails, or adequately powered tests rule out the preregistered benefit, reject or retain an inconclusive scoped hypothesis. Preserve failures and `h < 1`.
